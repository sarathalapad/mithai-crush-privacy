Mithai Crush Privacy Policy

Mithai Games · Effective date: 30 October 2026

Summary

  • Mithai Crush is a puzzle game intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not intended for children under 13. You do not need an account, a login, an email address or a phone number to play.
  • The game itself does not ask for, collect or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device. The game needs an internet connection to play: if the device goes offline, play pauses until the connection is back.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised, and the ad SDK can use the Android Advertising ID. Where a consent message applies, your choice decides what is allowed (see section 3).
  • If online features are switched on, an anonymous player ID is used to back up progress and show a stars leaderboard (see section 4).
  • Google and its advertising partners receive device data, including the Advertising ID, in order to show ads (see section 3).
  • There are no in-app purchases, no chat and no way for players to talk to each other.

1. Who we are

Mithai Crush (Android package com.mithaigames.mithaimela) is published by Mithai Games ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or about your data, contact us at alapadsarath@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following on your device only, so that you can continue where you left off: levels reached, stars, in-game coins ("Mohurs"), lives and life timer, boosters, daily quest / win streak / spin state, event and collection progress, a nickname picked from a fixed word list, settings (language, speed, sound, music, vibration, screen shake, reduced motion), and two ad counters (how many rewarded ads were watched toward the next life, and how many times the game was launched). This data never leaves your device unless the optional online features in section 4 are active. The rivals on the weekly board and in events are computer-generated characters created on your device, not other players. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. The game turns off Android cloud backup of this data.

While it is running, the game checks the internet connection every few seconds by contacting Google web addresses (clients3.google.com, then www.gstatic.com if the first does not answer). This check contains no game data and no personal information. Like any internet connection, it shows the device's IP address to the server it contacts.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mithai Crush is free and is supported by ads delivered through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, by Google LLC). No other ad network or mediation SDK is used.

How ads appear

  • A banner ad is shown at the bottom of the screen on every screen of the game, including during a level. It is hidden while a full-screen ad is showing.
  • A full-screen ad may appear each time the game is started, including the very first launch after installing (never when returning to the game from the background).
  • A full-screen ad may appear after a level ends. These full-screen ads never appear in the middle of a level.
  • Optional rewarded ads: the player chooses to watch an ad for a reward (part of an extra life, extra moves or time for one or two ads, a free helper before a level, or double coins after a win). During a level, they are offered only when the level would otherwise end. Nothing is granted if the ad is not completed.

Personalised ads

  • Ads may be personalised. This means Google and its advertising partners may use the Advertising ID and information about activity in apps and on websites to choose ads they expect to interest you, and to measure them.
  • Ad content is limited to a maximum rating of "T" (teen).

Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox

  • The game includes the Android Advertising ID permission, so the ad SDK can read the device advertising identifier. The game's own code does not read or store it.
  • The game also includes the Android Privacy Sandbox permissions that come with the ad SDK, so the SDK can use those ad features on devices that support them.
  • You can reset or delete the Advertising ID, and manage Privacy Sandbox ad settings where your device has them, in Android Settings. The menu names vary by device.

Your consent

  • Before ads start, the game checks with Google's User Messaging Platform whether a consent message applies to you, for example in the European Economic Area or the UK. If one applies, it is shown when you start the game, and your choice decides whether ads may be personalised or shown.
  • Some US states give residents rights over the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising, and the way personalised ads work may fall under those terms. Where Google's message for those states applies, it offers the choice that message describes.
  • Where no consent message applies, you may not be asked, and ads may be personalised. The Android Settings above remain available to you.
  • You can change your choice later in the game: open Settings, then Ad privacy choices.

What the ad SDK may process

To deliver, personalise and measure ads, count impressions and prevent fraud, the ad SDK and ad networks may automatically process information such as: the Advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (which can indicate an approximate region), device make and model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, app version, and ad interaction events (for example that an ad was shown, closed or clicked), plus crash and performance diagnostics of the SDK. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Optional online features (Unity Gaming Services)

The game contains optional online features from Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies). They only run when the game build is connected to our Unity Cloud project and the device is online. Without them the game plays normally and your progress stays on your device; an internet connection is still needed to play.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you for personal information such as your name or email in the game, and it has no sign-up or login screens.
  • No in-app purchases, no real-money payments, no chat, no friend lists and no user-generated content.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise (GPS) location.

The game is built with the Unity engine (Unity Technologies), which may send basic technical information about the device and the app to Unity.

6. Age

Mithai Crush is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not intended for children under 13. The game does not ask for your age. If you believe a child under 13 is playing or has given us personal information (for example by emailing us), contact us at alapadsarath@gmail.com and we will delete any such information we hold. A parent or guardian can also reset or delete the device's Advertising ID and uninstall the game.

7. Data security, retention and deletion

  • Data sent to Unity services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • On-device data is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android Settings.
  • Online progress and leaderboard data (if used) are kept while the game is offered, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • To delete online data linked to your device, email alapadsarath@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Mithai Crush data". Because we hold no names or emails, we may ask for the approximate date you last played to find the anonymous record; we delete it within 30 days.
  • Data held by Google and ad networks for advertising, fraud prevention and reporting is handled under their own retention rules. We do not hold it, so please use the Android Settings in section 3 and Google's privacy controls for it.

8. Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Contact alapadsarath@gmail.com. You can also use the consent and Android Settings options in section 3 and uninstall the game at any time. Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you further rights.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change adds new kinds of data collection, we will also update the Google Play listing and, where required, ask for consent first.
