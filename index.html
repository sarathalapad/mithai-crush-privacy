Mithai Crush Privacy Policy

Mithai Games · Effective date: 24 October 2026

Summary

  • Mithai Crush is a puzzle game for children and families. You do not need an account, a login, an email address or a phone number to play.
  • The game itself does not ask for, collect or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device. The game works fully offline.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are configured as child-directed and non-personalised: no interest-based ads, no remarketing, and the game does not use the Android Advertising ID.
  • If online features are switched on, an anonymous player ID is used to back up progress and show a stars leaderboard (see section 4).
  • We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not share data for advertising profiles.
  • There are no in-app purchases, no chat and no way for players to talk to each other.

1. Who we are

Mithai Crush (Android package com.mithaigames.mithaimela) is published by SarathKumar.K ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or about your child's data, contact us at alapadsarath@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following on your device only, so that you can continue where you left off: levels reached, stars, in-game coins ("Mohurs"), lives and life timer, boosters, daily quest / streak / spin state, settings (sound, music, vibration), and two ad counters (how many rewarded ads were watched toward the next life, and how many times the game was launched, used to skip the ad at the very first launch). This data never leaves your device unless the optional online features in section 4 are active. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. The game turns off Android cloud backup of this data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mithai Crush is free and is supported by ads delivered through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, by Google LLC), which takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. No other ad network or mediation SDK is used.

How ads appear

  • A full-screen ad may appear when the game is started (never on the very first launch after installing, and never when returning to the game from the background).
  • A full-screen ad may appear after a level ends. Full-screen ads never appear in the middle of a level.
  • Optional rewarded ads: the player chooses to watch an ad for a reward (part of an extra life, +5 moves, a free helper before a level, or double coins after a win). Nothing is granted if the ad is not completed.

How ads are configured for children

  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed (COPPA / "tag for child-directed treatment") and as under the age of consent, and are non-personalised (personalisation is switched off for every request).
  • The game removes the Android Advertising ID permission, so the ad SDK cannot read the device advertising identifier.
  • Ad content is limited to a general-audience ("G") rating, and sensitive ad categories are blocked in the AdMob settings.

What the ad SDK may process

To deliver and measure ads, count impressions and prevent fraud, the ad SDK and ad networks may automatically process technical information such as: IP address (which can indicate an approximate region), device make and model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, app version, and ad interaction events (for example that an ad was shown, closed or clicked), plus crash and performance diagnostics of the SDK. In child-directed mode this information is used only for contextual (non-personalised) ads, frequency capping, reporting, security and fraud prevention, not to build advertising profiles. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Optional online features (Unity Gaming Services)

The game contains optional online features from Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies). They only run when the game build is connected to our Unity Cloud project and the device is online. Without them the game is fully playable offline.

FeatureWhat is usedWhy
Anonymous sign-in (Unity Authentication)A random player ID and session token created by Unity. No name, email, phone number or social login is used.To link your saved progress and leaderboard score to this device's installation.

Like any online service, Unity's servers also receive the IP address and basic device information when these features connect.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not ask children (or anyone) for personal information, and the game has no sign-up or login screens.
  • No in-app purchases, no real-money payments, no chat, no friend lists and no user-generated content.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise (GPS) location.
  • No sale or rental of data. No interest-based (behavioural) advertising and no remarketing.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK of our own.

6. Children's privacy

Mithai Crush is designed for children and families and takes part in Google Play's Families program. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR rules for children's data, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and Google Play's Families policies, by not collecting personal information from children in the game and by configuring all ads as child-directed and non-personalised. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information (for example by emailing us), contact us at alapadsarath@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Data security, retention and deletion

  • Data sent to Unity services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • On-device data is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android Settings.
  • Online progress and leaderboard data (if used) are kept while the game is offered, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • To delete online data linked to your device, email alapadsarath@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Mithai Crush data". Because we hold no names or emails, we may ask for the approximate date you last played to find the anonymous record; we delete it within 30 days.
  • Data held by ad networks for fraud prevention and reporting is handled under their own retention rules.

8. Parents' rights

Parents and guardians can ask us at any time what information is held about their child, ask us to delete it, and ask us to stop any further processing. Contact alapadsarath@gmail.com. You can also reset the device's ad settings and uninstall the game at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change adds new kinds of data collection, we will also update the Google Play listing and, where required, ask for consent first.
